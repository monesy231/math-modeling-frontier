# Step 5 Spec: Isolated Press-Conference Writing & Paper Aesthetics

> **Core Calibration**: Concrete `❌ → ✅` contrasts define exact tone and typesetting craft without restricting mathematical structure.

## 1. Press-Conference Prose (Concrete Anti-Defensive Contrasts)
- **D1 Prebuttal**: ❌ *"We emphasize that / It should be noted that our model does not capture every real factor..."* → ✅ *"The coupled ODE tracks thermal flux across three boundary regimes."*
- **D2/D3 Hedge Stacking**: ❌ *"Although simplified, results may potentially suggest some improvement for reference only."* → ✅ *"Test RMSE drops 14.2% (95% CI: [12.8%, 15.6%], $p<0.001$)."*
- **D4 Lab Diary**: ❌ *"We first tried exact MIP, which failed to converge, so we reluctantly switched to relaxation."* → ✅ *"Convex relaxation solves $10^4$-node instances in 1.8 s within a 1.7% optimality gap."*
- **D5 Scattered Apologies**: ❌ Repeating caveats at the end of Sec. 2, 3, and 4 → ✅ Keep Results direct; consolidate quantitative validity boundaries (`valid for perturbation <= 12%`) + concrete math extensions in one final *Strengths, Weaknesses & Extensions* section.

## 2. Top-Prize Structural & Math Discipline
- **Coherent Spine**: Present `Q1 → Qn` as one evolving framework (white-box core → coupled/stochastic upgrade → sensitivity stress-test → policy lever), not isolated algorithm silos.
- **Operational Assumptions**: ❌ *"Assume the provided data is accurate."* → ✅ State `Simplification + Physical/Empirical Justification + Exact Eq. Hook + Relaxation Test`.
- **Math-First & Cross-Artifact Zero-Transcription Binding**: Give 1–2 lines of intuition before key equations; never silently alter conditions, quantifiers, inequalities, or units when polishing; match `00_plan.md` Nomenclature 100%. Use `\input{generated_metrics.tex}` + `generated_tables/*.tex` from `sync_metrics.py` (LaTeX), or load-time reconciliation assertions in `build_report.py` / `dashboard.py` verifying that `submission_policy`, row counts, and headline scalars match across all `03_outputs/metrics/*.json` before rendering a single page.
- **Theory-to-Online Bridge, Relaxation Scope & Dual-Cohort Accounting**: ❌ Stating an offline theorem with strict inequality (`>`) when a tied swap yields `0` net change, calling a restricted-cohort calculation a global "lower bound" when solutions cross period boundaries, or conflating item cohorts with ticket/entity cohorts → ✅ Verify theorems against bidirectional boundary counterexamples (weighting net indicators by evaluation-cohort membership), compute relaxation bounds over the open cross-period feasible domain (stating relaxed constraints and unshipped remainder), distinguish single-timestamp static first-deadline snapshots from dynamic online bounds, and report pre/eval/post carryover flow tables alongside full-horizon tail metrics.
- **Code-to-Prose Exactness, Ablations & Chronological Scope**: ❌ Misstating baseline tie-break key order, labeling a multi-switch policy step as a single-factor ablation, equating an inspired heuristic with a theorem, or omitting chronological split caveats → ✅ Match baseline tie-break keys, 1-flag-1-mechanism vs. cumulative ladder labels (synced across manuscript and dashboard), and theorem vs. inspired-heuristic scope verbatim to code; state exact sampling unit/law for every interval; disclose retrospective candidate-family reuse and straddling boundary counts in chronological lock-then-replay splits.
- **Dual Metric Clocks & Priority Honesty**: ❌ Hiding an unfavorable secondary official metric clock or silently picking a compromise over the strict priority winner → ✅ Report all competing official metric clocks upfront (Abstract, Summary Sheet, Main Table, Pareto frontiers) when prompt rules and workbook notes diverge, display the strict official-priority winner alongside any chosen business trade-off with exact objective deltas, and verify non-dominance + paired stress stability across the full candidate pool.
- **Abstract (Write LAST, Zero Fluff)**: ❌ *"We build an optimization model and find the optimal policy with good robustness."* → ✅ *"A daily cap of 14,200 visitors cuts peak ecological stress by 34.6% (95% CI: [31.9%, 37.2%]) while preserving 89.4% net revenue; thresholds shift <3.8% under ±10% elasticity perturbation."*
- **Hard Page Budget, Safe Math Rendering & Companion Templates**: Stay strictly within `00_plan.md`'s hard page limit (counting embedded companion sheets) by citing supplementary files instead of overflowing, and build PDFs atomically via staged tempfiles. In script-based PDF builders, **substitute all math symbols (`\in`, `\le`, Greek) BEFORE stripping font wrappers (`\mathcal`, `\mathrm`, `\mathbf`) and assert `"\\" not in expr`** so tokens like `\in\mathcal{I}` never leak as `\inI`; provide cross-platform font fallbacks (`UnicodeCIDFont` / bundled TTF + env override). Official companion sheets (`作品说明单` / `AI协作记录`): auto-generate from audited `metrics/` (`<=60`-char summary, strip all `> ...` prompts and `待汇总` deferrals, reconcile AI tool versions/costs/hours with git history).

## 3. Journal Paper & Interactive Dashboard Aesthetics (Typography, Tables & Palette)
- **Booktabs 3-Line Tables Only**: ❌ Vertical lines `|` and Excel-style boxed grids → ✅ `\toprule`, `\midrule`, `\bottomrule` only; left-align text, right-align numbers with uniform decimals, units in column headers, **bold** the best model, report `mean ± std` or `[95% CI]`.
- **Clean Math, Page Rhythm & Academic Ink-Navy Palette**: ❌ Muddy green/teal headings, table borders, zebra rows, or dashboard banners (`#153940`, `#009E73`, `#eef5f2`) → ✅ Use **neutral charcoal-ink & Oxford slate-navy** (`#111827` / `#1E293B` for titles, H2, and Booktabs rules; `#1E3A5F` / `#2B4C7E` for H3/code/UI accents; `#1A1A1A` body ink; `#475569` captions; `#F8FAFC` / `#F1F5F9` cool slate surfaces and table shading). Use upright `\mathrm{}` for non-variable subscripts and units (`14.2\,\mathrm{m/s}`); avoid 20+ line unbroken walls of text; embed native-sized figures at `\linewidth`.

## 4. Originality, Anti-AI-Slop & Zero Leaks
- **Paraphrase Hygiene**: Never copy prompt wording into Problem Restatement; reformulate crisply in mathematical prose.
- **Ban AI Clichés (Stay Rigorous, Not Chatty)**: Cut `With the rapid development of`, `It is well known`, `crucial/pivotal role`, `delve into`, `comprehensive framework`, `随着……飞速发展`, `众所周知`, `毋庸置疑`, `综上所述……重要意义`, unquantified `significantly`, and 3+ consecutive parallel triplets.
- **Zero Internal Leaks**: Never include `Gate`, `handoff.md`, `SKILL.md`, `00_plan`, `01_log`, `metrics.json`, `Subagent`, `3-Attempt`, `L2`, or `[F]/[W]` anywhere in text, tables, or LaTeX comments. *(Log AI usage ONLY inside an official appendix if required by `00_problem/`.)*
